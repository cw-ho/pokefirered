# HUSH — Plot Draft v2 (hardboiled)

> *On this island everybody carries the same badge. Doesn't mean a thing. I should know. I've had mine twelve years.*

v1 was a sad story about a decent man. v2 is a hardboiled one: the hero is dirty, he talks like it, he does ugly things to stay alive, the people who help him all want something, and no ending comes free.
Names are working names; the hero and rival can be renamed by the player.

---

## What Changed from v1

| v1 | v2 |
|---|---|
| Marcus is a passive, sad driver | Marcus is a **debt collector and ring fighter**: hard, cynical, good at hurting people |
| Things happen *to* him | He makes bad choices on purpose, including **selling out a friend** |
| Narration is neutral | **First-person voice-over** in Marcus's head: short, bitter, funny |
| Helpers are good people | **Everyone wants something.** The cop is skimming. The tech girl is working for the other side. |
| Violence off-screen | Beatings, hospital beds, a body in a canal. Shown, briefly, not dwelt on. |
| Kai is nice | Kai is nice **until it costs him**, then he picks his career |
| The Daylight ending is warm | Every ending **costs Marcus something he can't get back** |

---

## The Voice

Hardboiled lives in the narration. Between scenes, the screen goes dark and Marcus talks. In-game these are short text boxes on a black screen, or over a still map.

> *Four-forty in the morning. The kind of hour where the only people awake are bakers, cops and men like me. The bakers have the best of it.*

> *Kai looked good on the poster. Kai always looked good. Some people are born under a lucky star. Kai was born under a sponsorship deal.*

> *The Link at dawn. Two hundred thousand people crossing to make a living, and one of them carrying enough to hang him. Nobody looks at anyone. That's the deal.*

> *Pereira said trust was a luxury. He was right. He was also the one who couldn't afford it.*

> *I'd been hit before. The trick is to stay down long enough that they get bored, and get up before they get creative.*

Rules: short sentences. Concrete details. Jokes that aren't really jokes. Never explain feelings; describe what he does instead.

---

## The World (brief, as v1)

- **Halcyon / the Island**: clean, orderly, watching. Carry over the threshold and you don't come back.
- **Across**: the mainland. Marcus was born there and left at nine. Reachable in stages: **Bridgehead** (the border town) from Act 1, the **industrial zone** in Act 2, **Low's territory** in Act 4.
- **The Link**: the causeway. Open, crossed by hundreds of thousands a day. Where the chases happen.
- **Hush**: etomidate. A legal Pokémon sedative from **Silvane Pharmaceuticals**, sold on the street in vape pods.
- **Boxline**: the company that runs the PC network. Owned by Low Holdings. Every transfer scanned, unless you're on the whitelist.
- **Physical route** (Yeo's Harbour Line: lorries, boats) vs. **digital route** (the Kingfishers: held items on mule Pokémon, rented boxes). Both answer to the same man.

## Cast

**Marcus** (26). Quit at fourteen with one badge when his dad stopped sending money and the debts came for his mum. Now works for **Vincent Yeo**: drives when they need a driver, **collects debts** when they need a collector. Fights in the Lantern Row rings on weekends and has never lost one he was paid to win. He has lost a few he was paid to lose. Lies to his mum about all of it.

**Growlithe**. Twelve years old, one torn ear, never evolved. The rings have made it vicious. The one creature that's never once asked Marcus what he does for a living.

**Kai Lim**. Same block, same day, same badge. Silvane scholarship, Champion, the League's **Anti-Drug Ambassador**. Decent in the way people are when it's never cost them anything. **When it finally does, he chooses himself.** Comes back to it later, maybe.

**Inspector Leonard Pereira**. Narcotics. Twenty-nine years in, eleven months from his pension. Flips Marcus off the books because his department leaks, and because Pereira has been **skimming seized Hush** and selling it back through a middleman for years. He wants Yeo's routes shut so his own little business stays safe. He also, in his own way, likes Marcus. Both things are true.

**Priya Nair**. PC technician. Sharp, funny, the only person Marcus talks to like a person. Shows him the digital route and helps him trace it. **She works for Boxline's security, and for Low.** Her job is to steer Marcus into destroying Yeo's physical route, so the digital one can take over without a war. She likes him too. It doesn't change anything.

**Vincent Yeo**. Old-school boss. Paid for Marcus's mum's hospital bills. Never lets him forget it, never mentions it.

**Sam Rahman**. Yeo's dispatcher, Marcus's oldest friend in the life. Taught him to drive, to fight, and when to keep his mouth shut. **Marcus sells him out** in Act 3. Sam never sells Marcus.

**Nadia Hassan**. Mangrove Bay Gym Leader. Undercover, different unit. Tough, tired, nobody's love interest. Almost shoots Marcus once. Later, the only one who doesn't lie to him.

**Professor Raymond Seah**. Gave both boys their first Pokémon. Developed the sedative formula. Signs off on stock numbers he knows are wrong. Drinks.

**Deputy Commissioner Adrian Teo**. Runs the anti-drug campaign. Decides which raids go public and who gets the credit. **The one calling from Pereira's phone.**

**Edmund Low**. Across. Owns the ferry, the depot, Boxline, half of Silvane, and the Strait Cup. Patient, reasonable, and never once raises his voice. The worst man in the story, and the most polite.

**Mum**. Thinks Marcus works night shifts at a warehouse. Half believes it.

## Prologue: "Same Day"

- **Playable, age 14.** Marcus and Kai beat the Fort Calder Gym on the same afternoon. Kai's mum takes their photo.
- Voice-over, older Marcus: *"Same block. Same day. Same badge. Took me twelve years to work out the difference between us. Kai had somebody paying."*
- Cut to black. **Twelve years later.**

## Act 1: Collections (Badges 2–3)

1. **Opening job.** Marcus and Sam collect a debt from a man in a Greywater shophouse. The player battles the man's Pokémon, and then Marcus does the rest off-screen. The man's daughter watches from the stairs. *"Nothing personal. That's what makes it work."*
2. **The Link.** Dawn crossing to Bridgehead with a sealed van. The officer who waves Yeo's lorries through. Breakfast at a stall he's eaten at for years. He drives past the street he grew up on, every day, and never turns down it.
3. **The rings.** Lantern Row. Marcus is told to lose in round three. He wins anyway, because the other guy talked about his mum. Yeo has him held down and docked a month's pay. *"Ego is expensive, Marcus. Ask anyone in a cemetery."*
4. **Darren.** A fifteen-year-old from his block collapses at a bus stop with a pod in his hand. Marcus recognises the carton. He drove it.
5. **Pereira.** Picks Marcus up off Ashgrove Road at 2am. Doesn't threaten him. Shows him a photo of Mum leaving the hospital, and a file showing who paid the bill. *"You work for me now, or I make sure Yeo hears you've been talking anyway. Your choice. That's a joke. You don't have one."*
6. **The badge case.** Pereira wants him inside the Gyms, the clinics and the private parties. A trainer on the League circuit walks in anywhere. Marcus takes the case out of the drawer. *"Twelve years. The badge hadn't changed. I had."*
7. **Lumen Vista Gym (Badge 2).** The heiress Leader's brother is a user. She gives Marcus the badge and a name: her brother's dealer. *"Do something about it and I'll never ask what."* He does something about it.
8. **Pasir Quay Gym (Badge 3).** The union boss. Marcus wins, and the boss tells him which container to look at, for a price that will come due later.
9. **The bust.** Marcus tips Pereira. Container raid. On TV the next morning: *"Champion Kai Lim joins officers in record seizure."* Kai describes the raid at a podium.

**Act 1 ends.** Voice-over: *"Somewhere in a nice flat, Kai was watching himself on the news. I was watching from a coffee shop with a split lip. We both thought he looked good."*

## Act 2: The Middle of Nowhere (Badges 4–6)

1. **Kai comes home.** Charity event in Ashgrove. Kai hugs him, offers him a job in the League office. Marcus says no. Kai looks at his knuckles and doesn't ask.
2. **Lantern Row Gym (Badge 4).** The KTV owner fronts for Yeo. Marcus is promoted: no more driving, now he's in the room.
3. **Priya.** A Pokémon Center PC glitches. The technician shows Marcus a box of identical Rattata, all holding the same item, filed as clinic-to-clinic medical transfers. She's funny and smart, and she doesn't flinch at his face. He trusts her faster than he should.
4. **Kingfisher hit.** His van gets hit in Bridgehead. **Chase** through the industrial zone and back over the Link, Kingfisher riders behind, checkpoint officers ahead. Growlithe badly hurt. **Across's industrial zone opens.**
5. **Darren's funeral.** Mercy Hill columbarium. Mum is there. Kai sends a wreath with the Anti-Drug campaign logo on it. Marcus takes the card off the wreath and keeps it.
6. **Mangrove Bay Gym (Badge 5).** Nadia waves Yeo's night boat through. Their battle is ugly. Afterwards, on the boardwalk, she pulls a gun. He talks his way out, barely. Neither of them says who they work for.
7. **Meridian Central Gym (Badge 6).** The Silvane board member. Marcus gets onto the restricted floors and sees the stock numbers. Then he sees Pereira's name on a seizure log: half of what Pereira seized never reached evidence.
8. **The confrontation.** Marcus corners Pereira in a hawker centre at closing time. Pereira doesn't deny it. *"You think there are good guys? There are guys who get their pension and guys who don't. Help me get mine."*
9. **Greywater, three days later.** Pereira is found in a storm drain. Written up as a robbery. His phone is gone.

**Act 2 ends.** Voice-over: *"No file. No handler. No proof. Officially I was what I'd always been. The only man who could say different was in a drawer at the morgue, and they don't take visitors."*

## Act 3: Debts (Badges 7–8, Elite Four)

1. **Pereira's phone rings.** Marcus answers. A calm voice: *"Keep driving, Marcus. I'll tell you where."* It's Teo. Marcus is a puppet with a new hand up his back.
2. **The squeeze.** Teo wants a name inside Yeo's organisation to hang the next big bust on. Kingfishers break into Mum's flat as a warning to the Harbour Line. Mum ends up in hospital.
3. **The sellout.** Marcus gives Teo **Sam's name.** Sam is arrested in a televised raid, credited to Kai. In the interview room Teo offers Sam a deal: name the informant and walk. **Sam says nothing.** Marcus watches the news from Mum's hospital bed. *"I'd like to tell you I felt bad about it. I did. It didn't change a thing, and that's the whole problem with feeling bad."*
4. **Nadia.** She finds Marcus. Her unit has been watching Teo for a year. Truths on both sides, finally. She doesn't forgive him for Sam. She works with him anyway.
5. **Sera Island Gym (Badge 7).** Prof. Seah, drunk at the abandoned research station, hands over the original formula records and a Fire Stone he bought twenty years ago for a boy who quit. *"I kept waiting for you to come back. I should've come looking."*
6. **Priya.** Marcus traces the whitelist back through the logs, and it leads to Priya's login. She was steering him all along: every lead hit Yeo, never the Kingfishers. Rooftop, rain. She doesn't apologise. *"You were going to burn something down anyway. I just pointed."* He lets her walk. That's a choice; the game remembers it.
7. **Yeo falls.** The Harbour Line is wiped out in one night of raids, all credited to Kai. Yeo gets one phone call from his cell. He uses it on Marcus. *"I paid for your mother's bed. I'd do it again. Tell me that means nothing."*
8. **Greywater Gym (Badge 8).** The checkpoint commander. The last badge.
9. **Kai.** Marcus finally tells him everything, with the wreath card, the seizure logs and the whitelist. Asks him to go public. Kai is quiet for a long time. *"If I say this, everything I've done is a lie."* Marcus: *"It is."* **Kai walks away.**
10. **The Elite Four.** Clean, fair fights, the first in the game. Except the third member, who runs the sponsorship office: his Pokémon don't flinch, don't feel pain, and their eyes are glassy. Sedated.
11. **The Champion.** Kai, who shows up anyway. No speeches. If Growlithe has the Fire Stone, it evolves at the start of the fight.

**Act 3 ends.** Marcus wins. The League says the result is under review and Kai remains Champion. That night an envelope arrives at Mum's empty flat: the **Strait Cup invitation**. *"Mr. Low would very much like to meet you."*
Voice-over: *"Eight badges. Took me twelve years and two friends. Turns out the prize for beating the system is a job offer."*

---

## Act 4: Across (final act)

1. **The crossing.** Same Link, same dawn queue, but in a League car with a driver of his own. Past Bridgehead, past the industrial zone. **Low's territory opens.**
2. **His father.** Nights at a Boxline server farm. Grey, shaking, a Hush user. He recognises Marcus straight away and asks him for money. Marcus gives it to him. *"I'd spent twelve years being angry at a man who didn't exist. The real one was worse. He was just tired."*
3. **Kai crosses.** Uninvited. Says he's there for the exhibition. He isn't.
4. **Edmund Low.** Lunch at the top of the ferry terminal. Low knows everything: Pereira, Priya, Sam, the bed Yeo paid for. *"You know both routes. You beat the Champion. You sold your best friend to keep your mother safe. Marcus, you're the most honest man I've met in years."* He offers Marcus the Island.
5. **The data centre.** The whitelist, the mule accounts, the transfer logs: the evidence and the business are the same machine. Kingfishers, Low's security, and Teo's men (off the books, across the border) close in. Nadia's unit can't cross. Kai is the only cop on the wrong side of the Link, with no jurisdiction, and he stays.

## Endings (all cost something)

Decided by choices across the game: whether you used Hush on Growlithe in the rings, what you did with Priya, whether you protected Nadia, what you told Kai, and your answer to Low.

### Daylight
Marcus gets the logs out and hands them to Kai. This time **Kai goes public**: he names Teo, the sponsorship office and Low, and says the credit for every bust of the last two years belongs to an informant. Kai loses everything: title, sponsors, career.
Marcus is arrested at the Link with his own record. Too many crossings, too many boxes. He testifies. He gets time anyway.
Final scene: a prison property room. A guard logs his belongings: *"One badge case. Eight badges."* Voice-over: *"Kai visits on Sundays. We don't talk about it. We talk about the old block, and who's dead. It's a long list. We're not on it. That'll do."*

### Infernal
Marcus gets the logs out, and they disappear. Teo retires with honours. Low's lawyers do their job. Kai stays Champion and gives a speech about a cleaner League.
Final scene: 4:40am. A new depot, a new dispatcher, a sealed box. Marcus looks at it for a long time. Then he takes it.
Voice-over: *"Same badge. Same block. Same day. Everybody gets the ending they can afford."*

### Boss
Marcus takes Low's offer. Priya runs his systems. Nadia's unit is quietly disbanded.
Final scene: an office in Meridian Central, a year later. His phone rings. Kai, still the Champion: *"What do you need me to say this time?"*
Voice-over: *"I used to wonder what it'd be like on the poster. Turns out I'm the one who prints them."*

## Tone Rules

- **Nobody is clean**, including the hero. The player should wince at least once at something Marcus does.
- **Every favour is a debt.** Every helper has an angle.
- **Violence is quick and ugly**, then it's over. No gore; it's the aftermath that matters: the hospital, the empty chair, the funeral.
- **The voice-over carries the tone.** If a scene feels soft, give Marcus a line.
- **No speeches about drugs.** Show the bus stop, the funeral, the father.
